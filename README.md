## ✨ Features

- **30 daily lessons** covering the full A1 (CEFR) syllabus – 4 weeks + exam week
- **663 words** with article colours, plural forms and English + Bangla meanings
- **Audio everywhere** – tap any underlined German word, sentence or dialogue to hear it
- **Play whole dialogues** line by line, with different voices for each speaker
- **Interactive exercises** – multiple choice, fill-in-the-gap, translation and writing tasks with instant checking
- **Weekly tests** (Days 7, 14, 21, 28) and a **final test** (Day 30)
- **Exam training** for Goethe-Zertifikat A1 / telc Deutsch A1 (Day 29)
- **Flashcards** for every day and for all words
- **All words** page with search and filters (by week, day and article)
- **Grammar secrets** page with short cuts for articles, plurals, cases and the perfect tense
- **Syllabus page (Kursplan)** with all 30 days and a grammar map
- **Language switch:** English / বাংলা / both
- **Progress tracking** – completed days and best scores are saved in your browser
- **Umlaut keyboard** (ä ö ü ß) for typing answers on any phone
- **Fully responsive** – layout checked at phone (360 px), tablet (768 px) and desktop (1280 px) widths, with no sideways scrolling; large tap targets, print-friendly pages
- **🌐 Translator** – German → Bangla, German → English, English → German, Bangla → German (also English ↔ Bangla). For every word you get the **article (der / die / das)**, gender, plural and **example sentences**
- **📖 A1 Dictionary A–Z** – all course words and phrases in alphabetical order with article, plural, Bangla and English meaning (German → Bangla / German → English / both), search in any language, filter by der / die / das, example sentences on tap
- **🔁 Wiederholung** – `#review`: spaced-repetition word cards (course book + topic words, with articles and plural); known words return later, forgotten ones soon; daily streak.
- **🎧 Hörverstehen** – `#listen`: listening practice with the browser voice; text hidden until you answer.
- **🎓 Prüfungs-Simulator** – `#exam`: 40-minute mock of the A1 exam (Hören, Lesen, Grammatik, then Schreiben and Sprechen tasks with model answers).
- **📘 Kursbuch A1 (course book companion)** – `#book`: the 12 chapters of the Netzwerk A1 book (Guten Tag! … Ab in den Urlaub!) as study pages written in our own words: overview, grammar with examples, 8 words with articles, practice sentences with audio, a reading text, a chapter quiz with explanations, and Schreiben/Sprechen exam tasks with model answers. Plus 4 progress tests (`#booktest-1`…`4`) and a final 20-question mock exam (`#bookfinal`, pass mark 60%, best score saved).
- **🗂 Themen-Training (practice by topic)** – `#topics`: 8 everyday topics (Wohnung, Essen & Trinken, Arbeit & Beruf, Familie & Freunde, Einkaufen, Freizeit & Hobbys, Unterwegs, Beim Arzt). Each has 10 words with article and plural, 5 useful phrases, a short dialogue (audio + Bangla/English) and a 6-question quiz (meaning + der/die/das)
- **🧩 Leicht erklärt (easy guide & games)** – `#easy`: **article trainer** (der/die/das game with the ending rule after every answer), **sentence builder** (tap words into the right order), nicht vs. kein, modal verbs, separable verbs, prepositions, question words, numbers & time, the 20 key verbs, common mistakes, **A1 exam guide** (Goethe A1 format, speaking and writing models), a 15-minute daily plan and a 12-point **A1 self-test**
- **💬 Help chat** – ask anything about the website (plan, days, tests, audio, features, how to host …), look up a word with its article, or paste any German / English / Bangla text and get it translated into the other two languages automatically (optional free Gemini AI for a premium feel). Bangla, English and Banglish questions are understood
- **📷 Photo translation** – send **as many pictures as you like** (camera or gallery, several at once, paste or drag & drop). The German text in each picture is read (OCR) and translated into **Bangla** (English with one tap)
- **🎙 Voice input** – speak your question to the chat in বাংলা, English or Deutsch
- **One single file** – no installation, no server, works offline (the translator, photo reading and voice input need internet)

## 🚀 How to use

### Online
Open **[mdbellalhosen.github.io/German-A1](https://mdbellalhosen.github.io/German-A1/)** in **Chrome, Edge or Safari**. Start with Day 1 and do one lesson per day.

### Offline / on your computer
1. Download `index.html`
2. Double-click it – it opens in your browser
3. That's it – no installation needed

### Host it yourself (GitHub Pages)
1. Upload `index.html` to a public repository
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**
3. Choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch `main`, folder `/ (root)` and click **Save**
4. After 1–2 minutes your site is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY-NAME/`

## 🔊 Audio

The audio uses the **Web Speech API** – the text-to-speech engine that is built into your phone or computer. No internet audio files are needed, but your device must have a **German voice**.

| Device | How to get a German voice |
|---|---|
| **Android** | Install / update *Speech Services by Google* from the Play Store → Settings → Text-to-speech → Google → Install voice data → **Deutsch (Deutschland)** |
| **iPhone / iPad** | Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → **German** → download a voice (e.g. Anna). Turn off silent mode. |
| **Windows** | Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices → **Deutsch**. Microsoft Edge already has good German voices. |
| **Mac** | System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage voices → **German** |

**No sound?**
- Open the site in **Chrome or Safari** – not inside another app's built-in browser
- Turn up the **media volume**
- Tap ⚙️ (top right) → **Test** – the line under the button shows which German voice is used
- You can change the voice and the speed (0.75× is great for beginners) in ⚙️

## 🌐 Translator & 📖 Dictionary

Open **Übersetzer** (top right 🌐 icon, or `#translate`).

- Choose a direction with one tap: **Deutsch → বাংলা**, **Deutsch → English**, **English → Deutsch**, **বাংলা → Deutsch** (or pick any pair with the two menus, ⇄ swaps them)
- Type **any German, English or Bangla word** and you get the translation **plus the article** (coloured der = blue, die = red, das = green), gender and plural – and **4 example sentences** with English and Bangla translation
  - **Word in the A1 course** → article, plural, meanings and the lesson (Tag) come from the course; the example sentences are real sentences from the lessons, completed with model sentences if needed
  - **Word not in the course** → the word type and article (and plural) are looked up online in [German Wiktionary](https://de.wiktionary.org); if that is not possible the article is guessed from the ending and clearly marked as a guess
  - **Pronouns, verbs, adjectives …** have no article – the card says so and shows the word type, with example sentences
  - Model sentences are always grammatically safe patterns (e.g. *Das ist ein Tisch.* · *Wo ist der Tisch?* · *Ich brauche einen Tisch.* · *Wir haben zwei Tische.*) and are marked “model sentence”
- Type a sentence and you get the translation and, for German text, the course words in it with their articles
- The translation of words and sentences from the course is answered **instantly and offline** (example sentences that need a translation, and everything outside the course, need internet). Everything else is translated online ([Google Translate](https://translate.google.com) web endpoint, with [MyMemory](https://mymemory.translated.net) as a fallback)

**Wörterbuch A–Z** (`#dictionary`) lists every word and phrase of the course (663 entries, 363 of them nouns with article) from A to Z. Switch **Deutsch → বাংলা**, **Deutsch → English** or both, search in German, English or Bangla, filter by **der / die / das / Plural**, jump with the letter bar and tap **Examples** for sentences.

## 💬 Help chat, photos & voice

Tap the round chat button (bottom right).

| What | How |
|---|---|
| **Ask about the website** | “What is this site?”, “Day 8”, “Week 3”, “How do I start?”, “Audio not working”, “How do I host it?” … (English, বাংলা or Banglish) |
| **Look up a word** | “article of Tisch”, “টেবিল মানে কী”, “Haus” → article, plural, meanings, examples |
| **Translate anything (A–Z)** | Just **type or paste any word or paragraph** – the language is detected automatically: **German → Bangla + English**, **Bangla → German + English**, **English → German + Bangla**. Every result has a listen (German) and a copy button; single words also get the article and example sentences. Commands such as “translate ich bin müde to bangla” still work |
| **🌐 Auto / 🔤 Übersetzen** | The button next to the ä ö ü ß keys. *Auto*: questions about the site are answered, other text is translated. *Übersetzen*: **everything** you type is translated |
| **📷 Camera** | Phone: opens the camera app. Computer: live camera window – snap one page after another |
| **🖼 Photos** | Choose many pictures at once, or paste (Ctrl+V) / drag & drop them into the chat. **There is no limit** – pictures are processed one after another |
| **🎙 Voice** | Tap the microphone, speak, the question is sent automatically. Tap the language button next to the ä ö ü ß keys to choose বাংলা / English / Deutsch (Chrome or Safari) |

**How photo translation works:** the picture is shrunk and cleaned up in your browser, the German text is read with [Tesseract.js](https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js) (OCR, runs **on your device** – the first time it downloads about 15 MB, afterwards it is cached), and only the recognised **text** is sent to the online translator. The picture itself never leaves your device. You see the German text, the Bangla translation, a listen button, a copy button and **“Edit in translator”** to fix reading errors.

Tips for good results: good light, hold the page straight, fill the frame with the text, avoid shadows and blur. Printed books work best; handwriting does not.

### ✨ Premium AI (free) – optional

The chat works on its own (course data + free online translators). For a smarter, ChatGPT-like experience you can switch on **Google Gemini** at **no cost**: free-form questions, whole paragraphs, grammar explanations and **photo reading with AI vision** (better than the offline reader). The key stays secret on a small server function, [`api/chat.ts`](api/chat.ts) (TypeScript, Vercel). If the AI is not set up, busy or offline, the chat silently falls back to the free built-in engine – nothing breaks.

1. Get a free key: <https://aistudio.google.com/apikey> (no credit card).
2. Import this repository at <https://vercel.com/new> (free Hobby plan). Vercel detects `api/chat.ts` automatically.
3. In Vercel → *Settings → Environment Variables* add `GEMINI_API_KEY` (your key). Optional: `GEMINI_MODEL` (default `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`; Google retires older free models from time to time – if `/api\/chat` ever returns a “model … no longer available” error in `detail`, set `GEMINI_MODEL` to a current Gemini Flash model), `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` (default `https://mdbellalhosen.github.io`). Redeploy.
4. In `index.html` set `const AI_ENDPOINT=…` to your function URL (this repo already uses `https://german-a1-eight.vercel.app/api/chat`).
5. Check `https://YOUR-PROJECT.vercel.app/api/chat` in the browser – it shows `"configured": true`.

Good to know: the free Gemini tier has rate limits, so AI photo reading is not unlimited – when the limit is reached the chat uses the offline reader (Tesseract) automatically, which has no limit. On Google's free tier, messages may be used by Google to improve its products, so do not send private information. The function allows only your site, caps the size of messages/pictures and limits requests per visitor.

## 🗂 Project structure

Everything is inside **one file**: `index.html`

```
index.html
├── <style>    Design: colours, layout, mobile view
├── <script>   Course content: DAYS.push({ … }) – one block per day (Days 1–30)
└── <script>   App logic: audio engine, router, exercises, flashcards, word list, syllabus,
               translator, A–Z dictionary, help chat (auto translation, photo OCR, voice, camera)
api/chat.ts        Optional free AI proxy (TypeScript, Vercel Function → Google Gemini)
package.json, tsconfig.json, vercel.json   Config for the AI function only
```

Pages (hash routes): `#home` · `#review` · `#listen` · `#exam` · `#book` · `#book-1` … `#book-12` · `#booktest-1` … `#booktest-4` · `#bookfinal` · `#topics` · `#topic-wohnung` … · `#easy` · `#plan` · `#day-1` … `#day-30` · `#words` · `#secrets` · `#translate` · `#dictionary`

## ✏️ Edit or add content

Each day is one `DAYS.push({ … })` block. Search for `n:8` to find Day 8, for example.

```js
DAYS.push({n:31, w:5, s:"Short title",
  de:"German title", en:"English title", bn:"বাংলা শিরোনাম",
  goals:[["English goal","বাংলা লক্ষ্য"]],
  vocab:[["der Tisch","Tische","table","টেবিল"]],          // word, plural, English, Bangla
  gram:[{h:"Heading", en:"English text", bn:"বাংলা লেখা",
         tb:{h:["Col 1","Col 2"], r:[["a","b"]]},           // table
         ex:[["Deutsch","English","বাংলা"]]}],               // examples
  dlg:[{h:"Dialog title", l:[["Speaker","Deutsch","English","বাংলা"]]}],
  tip:[["English tip","বাংলা টিপস"]],
  ex:[
    {k:"mc",   h:["Task","কাজ"], q:[["Question ___",["a","b","c"],0]]},   // index of correct option
    {k:"fill", h:["Task","কাজ"], q:[["Ich ___ Rahim.","bin"]]},            // use | for alternatives
    {k:"tr",   h:["Task","কাজ"], q:[["English","বাংলা","Deutsch"]]},
    {k:"wr",   h:["Task","কাজ"], q:[["Task","কাজ","Model DE","Model EN","Model BN"]]}
  ],
  qa:[["Frage","Question","প্রশ্ন","Antwort","Answer","উত্তর"]],
  hw:["English homework","বাংলা বাড়ির কাজ"]
});
```

- `w` is the week number (5 = exam week)
- `review:true` marks a test day
- Vocabulary plural: `""` = not a noun, `"-"` = no plural, `"Pl."` = plural only

## 🛠 Built with

- HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript – no frameworks, no build step
- Web Speech API for audio (text-to-speech) and voice input (speech recognition)
- [Tesseract.js](https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js) (loaded from a CDN only when you send a photo) for reading German text in pictures
- Google Translate web endpoint / MyMemory API for online translation and [German Wiktionary](https://de.wiktionary.org) (articles and word types of words outside the course) – only used when the course has no answer
- TypeScript + [Vercel Functions](https://vercel.com/docs/functions) and the [Google Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/) (free tier) for the optional premium AI chat
- Google Fonts: Inter, Fraunces, Hind Siliguri
- `localStorage` for progress (stays in your own browser only)
